## Format

| id | kind | ref | topics |
|---|---|---|---|

- `id` — short stable slug, used in page `sources:` frontmatter
- `kind` — `repo` | `docs` | `catalog` | `list` | `article` | `gist` | `issue` | `copy`

- `topics` — comma-separated, free-form
